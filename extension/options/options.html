Vimeo Grab

Settings save automatically.

How to use it

  1. Open a page with a Vimeo video, then click the Vimeo Grab icon (or press Alt+Shift+V).
  2. Pick the videos and quality you want, then click Download.
  3. You can also right-click a Vimeo link, paste a link into the popup, or keep browsing: downloads continue in the background.

Tip: pin the extension from Chrome’s puzzle-piece menu so it’s one click away.

Downloads

“Up to” picks the best version at or below that size. Videos are saved without re-encoding.

Use slashes for subfolders, for example Vimeo/Courses.

About

Vimeo Grab runs entirely in your browser. It has no analytics, sends nothing to its authors, and only talks to Vimeo. It won’t download DRM-protected videos or anything you can’t already watch.

Please only download videos you have the right to save. Vimeo Grab isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Vimeo.
